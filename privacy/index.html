Local document processing

Save Your PUB Files processes Microsoft Publisher documents locally on your Windows PC. The desktop app does not upload PUB files or generated PDFs to the developer, and it does not require a user account.

Website data

The website uses Umami analytics to understand basic site usage, such as page visits and selected product actions including buy clicks, checkout openings, completed checkout events, and video plays. The site does not use advertising trackers or third-party advertising profiling. Basic technical, server, and security logs may also be processed by the hosting or CDN provider as part of serving and protecting the website.

Purchases

When purchasing is enabled, payment processing will be handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Paddle may collect and process buyer, payment, and contact information under Paddle's own privacy notice.

Once purchases are enabled, the developer may receive limited buyer information from Paddle where necessary for order fulfilment, support, fraud prevention, or other legitimate transaction-related purposes. The developer does not receive full card or payment details.

Support and privacy questions

Support messages may include personal data such as your email address, order information, device details, and the contents of your message. For privacy questions, contact Support.
