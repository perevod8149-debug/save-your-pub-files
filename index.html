Requirements and limitations

What Save Your PUB Files does - and does not do

Save Your PUB Files uses the copy of Microsoft Publisher already installed on your PC to convert PUB files to PDF. This gives you Publisher's own rendering for files Publisher can open.

It is not a replacement for Microsoft Publisher.

It is not mainly for converting one file. It is for batch conversion when you need to convert multiple PUB files, entire folders, or hundreds of Publisher files. It cannot convert a .pub file that your installed version of Publisher itself no longer supports or cannot open.

FAQ

Questions before you convert

Will my .pub files stop working on October 1, 2026?

The files still exist, but access changes. Microsoft says Microsoft 365 subscribers will no longer be able to access Publisher after October 1, 2026. Perpetual versions may continue to run, but Publisher will no longer be supported.

Does this work without Microsoft Publisher?

No. Save Your PUB Files requires a working licensed installation of Microsoft Publisher on the Windows PC doing the conversion.

Why not use an online converter?

Online converters require uploading your documents and are usually awkward for bulk conversion. Save Your PUB Files processes files locally and uses your installed copy of Publisher as the rendering engine.

Can I convert hundreds or thousands of files?

Yes. The app is designed to convert hundreds of Publisher files and other bulk conversion workflows. Scan a folder, include subfolders if needed, and convert the batch to PDF.

Will it change my original Publisher files?

No. Original PUB files are never modified, renamed, moved or deleted.

What happens if one file is damaged or too old?

The app reports that file as failed and continues with the rest of the batch.

Can it open very old Publisher files that my Publisher cannot open?

No. If your installed version of Microsoft Publisher cannot open a PUB file, Save Your PUB Files cannot recover or render it.

Can I keep using Publisher afterward?

You can continue using Publisher as long as your installed version remains available and working. The app simply helps create PDF copies of your Publisher archive.
