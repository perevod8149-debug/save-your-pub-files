When one-by-one conversion stops making sense

Opening 2 or 3 Publisher files and saving each one as a PDF is manageable. Opening 20 files is already tedious. Opening 200 files can take over your afternoon. Opening 2,000 files one by one is the kind of project most people put off until it becomes urgent.

Save Your PUB Files is made for ordinary Windows users who need to convert many PUB files without writing scripts, editing PowerShell commands, or uploading documents to an online converter.

Convert many Publisher files in one batch

With Save Your PUB Files, you select the Publisher files or folder you want to process, choose where the PDFs should go, and run the conversion as one batch. The app uses the working Microsoft Publisher installation on your Windows PC to create PDF copies.

This is useful whether you are cleaning up a small archive of 20 files, preserving a department folder with 200 files, or preparing a large archive with 2,000 PUB files before Publisher becomes harder to access.

Built for nontechnical users

You do not need to be a developer, and you do not need to use the command line. Save Your PUB Files gives you a normal Windows interface for the same practical job: turn lots of Publisher files into PDFs without opening each document manually.

The app is Windows only, requires Microsoft Publisher, and does not replace Publisher. It helps automate the export work while your installed copy of Publisher can still open the files.

FAQ

Can I turn hundreds of PUB files into PDFs at once?

Yes. Save Your PUB Files is designed for converting many Publisher files to PDF in one batch, as long as Microsoft Publisher is installed and can open those files.

Can I change thousands of Publisher files to PDF?

You can process large groups of Publisher files, including very large archives. The practical limit depends on your PC, your Publisher installation, and whether Publisher can open the files.

Do I have to open every PUB file individually?

No. The point of Save Your PUB Files is to avoid opening every file manually when you need PDF copies of many Publisher documents.
