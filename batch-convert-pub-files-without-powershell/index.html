Why PowerShell comes up

For many Publisher files, Microsoft recommends considering automation with a PowerShell script. That can be useful when you are comfortable with scripts, execution policies, file paths, and troubleshooting command-line errors.

Many people simply want to convert multiple Publisher files without turning the job into a scripting project.

A normal Windows workflow

Save Your PUB Files gives batch conversion a simple Windows interface: Choose folder, Scan, Convert. The goal is to convert an entire folder, including many files at once, without editing scripts.

It is built for bulk conversion and mass conversion scenarios such as converting hundreds of PUB files before Publisher support ends.

What it does and does not do

The app requires a working Microsoft Publisher installation on the Windows PC doing the conversion. It does not open PUB files without Publisher and does not include a standalone PUB rendering engine.

Your files stay local. The app scans the selected folder, reports what it finds, and uses your installed Publisher to create PDFs for files Publisher can open.
