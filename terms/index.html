Product scope

Save Your PUB Files is a Windows desktop utility for batch conversion of Microsoft Publisher files to PDF. A working, licensed installation of Microsoft Publisher is required. It is not a replacement for Microsoft Publisher.

The app can convert only files that your installed copy of Publisher can open. Very old, damaged, unsupported, protected, or otherwise unreadable files may fail.

Your files and backups

You remain responsible for your original files and backups. The app is designed not to modify or delete original PUB files and not to overwrite existing PDFs, but you should still maintain normal backups before batch processing important archives.
